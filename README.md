# Overturn 🛡️
### Autonomous Health Insurance Denial Appeal & Pre-Auth Strategist
> Built for **WCC Launchpad 30** (We Code Coders) — National 30-Hour Hackathon  
> **Track:** **01 — AGENTIC AI** *(with cross-cutting impact in Healthcare Operations & Everyday Automation)*

---

## 💡 The Problem
Every year, millions of legitimate health insurance pre-authorizations and medical claims are rejected using automated, opaque denial codes (*"Not medically necessary"*, *"Lacks conservative therapy proof"*, *"Coding mismatch"*). 

While **up to 80% of these denials are legally overturnable**, navigating 90-page Insurer Clinical Policy Bulletins (CPBs), cross-referencing messy hospital charts, and drafting formal legal-medical appeal packages takes **4–6 hours per claim**. Overwhelmed clinic billing staff and stressed patients simply give up and pay out of pocket.

---

## 🚀 The Solution: Overturn
**Overturn** is an agentic AI system that automates the entire claim denial defense journey while keeping healthcare professionals and patients firmly in control:

1. **Autonomous Ingestion & Rule Mapping:** Ingests rejection letters, extracts official denial codes (e.g. `PR-50`), and maps them directly to insurer clinical policy guidelines.
2. **Clinical Chart Cross-Audit:** Scans patient medical records to pinpoint the exact "smoking gun" evidence the insurer missed or ignored.
3. **Evidence Battle Board (Human-in-the-Loop):** Presents an interactive, side-by-side comparison of *What Insurer Claimed* vs *What the Medical Records Proved*, requiring explicit human sign-off.
4. **Statutory Appeal Dossier & Regulator Shield:** Generates an airtight, legally cited appeal packet referencing ICD-10/CPT codes, medical necessity criteria, and the statutory **15-Day IRDAI Redressal SLA** with 1-click Insurance Ombudsman escalation.

---

## ⚡ Key Differentiators & Secret Weapons
- 🎯 **Overturn Probability Meter:** Dynamic win probability gauge (e.g., 88% Win Rate) with weighted factor breakdown.
- 🕵️ **Insurer Trap Detector:** Flags unfair insurer loopholes (such as citing outdated clinical bulletins or miscalculating treatment durations).
- ⚖️ **Statutory Legal Shield:** Auto-attaches regulatory mandates (IRDAI Policyholder Protection Regulations / 15-day resolution clock).
- ⚡ **Zero-Friction Demo Mode:** Pre-loaded with 3 real-world clinical denial scenarios (Lumbar Spine MRI, Knee Arthroscopy, Diabetes CGM) for instant, crash-proof live judging.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend Framework:** React 19 + Vite (Fast HMR & sub-2-second production builds)
- **Language:** TypeScript (Strict typing for clinical claim schemas)
- **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons + Framer Motion (Smooth, accessible medical design)
- **Deployment & CI/CD:** Vercel with automatic GitHub continuous integration (`vercel.json`)
- **AI Engine:** Dual-mode architecture (Deterministic verified seed cases + Live LLM API bridge)
